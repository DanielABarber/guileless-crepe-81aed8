About this property

Cozy cottage on a sunny lot walkable to downtown Cortez, this well maintained house is a great home in Montezuma County. There are two spacious bedrooms and two full baths, a kitchen with a large window, and a comfortable living space. The property has a small shed on its expansive lot.

Inside, the layout is simple and easy to live in: the kitchen opens onto the main living area, and the two bedrooms sit toward the back of the house for some quiet distance from the street. The shed out back adds extra storage or workshop space beyond the house's own footprint.

The home was remodeled in 2008 and has been well kept since. Mature shade trees and a covered front porch make for an easy spot to sit outside, and it's a short walk into downtown Cortez from here. Reach out to Chris Barber to schedule a showing.

Location

Cortez, Colorado

Cortez sits in the high desert of southwest Colorado, in Montezuma County — mesa-and-canyon country at around 6,200 feet. It's the closest town to Mesa Verde National Park, and sits within the wider Four Corners region, near the Ute Mountain and Canyons of the Ancients areas. Durango is roughly an hour's drive east over the mountains.
